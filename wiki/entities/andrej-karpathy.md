# Andrej Karpathy

Founding member of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla. 

Karpathy is a key influence on the **LLM Wiki** pattern, specifically via his gist on building personal knowledge bases using LLMs.

## Contributions
- **LLM Wiki Pattern**: Conceptualized the "compiler vs retriever" approach to knowledge management.
- **Deep Learning Education**: Known for the CS231n course and the "Zero to Hero" series.
